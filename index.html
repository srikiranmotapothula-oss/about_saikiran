About

Healthcare has strict rules. The interface still has to feel simple.

I specialize in user flows, wireframing, high-fidelity UI and design systems for healthcare, insurance and hospital-marketing software. My work spans an employee health-benefits portal for NFC (Nuclear Fuel Complex), a patient-facing portal for MRR, and a hospital-marketing mobile app for GROH — all at Suvarna Technosoft.

I move between design and implementation: sketching flows and screens in Figma, then converting approved designs into responsive interfaces myself using HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap and PrimeNG, with basic Angular knowledge to stay aligned with development teams.
